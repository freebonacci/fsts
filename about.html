About

From Seed to Shelter is a volunteer-run initiative that began in 2025. We are deliberate about prioritising where we can make an impact and where we can learn the most. Our ambition is to grow into an organisation that works wherever basic needs are failing for displaced people, and a community-grounded approach can be found.
