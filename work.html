Our work

  • Greece · in progress

    Microgreens farming, Greece

    Refugees in Greece receive enough calories, but the food is low in quality and micronutrients. In 2025 we ran a low-tech microgreens farm at a community centre outside Zervou Camp on Samos.

    We have now moved towards a home-growing model. We have produced tutorial videos in 7 languages to demonstrate how anyone can grow microgreens with everyday materials, and are inviting other organisations to make this programme available to community members.

  • Uganda · in progress

    Food insecurity research, Uganda

    Uganda's refugee policy expects new arrivals to become self-reliant by farming the land they are given. That is getting harder. Aid cuts mean that, for most, food support now ends within three months, while a first harvest takes at least four. Plots have also shrunk to around 10 × 15m, too small to feed a family.

    We are most concerned about the negative coping mechanisms households turn to, and are researching how to reduce the vulnerability that leaves families facing impossible choices.
